Welcome to Oshneer View Guest House

Oshneer View Guest House is a peaceful destination in Parwak, Upper Chitral, surrounded by the natural beauty of the mountains.

Our goal is to provide guests with comfortable accommodation, delicious food, warm hospitality, and an authentic experience of Chitral.

Whether visitors are coming for nature, adventure, culture, photography, family travel, or simply a peaceful escape, Oshneer View provides a welcoming place to stay.

Rooms & Accommodation

Four rooms, each with two beds. Room prices range from PKR 6,000 to PKR 8,000.
